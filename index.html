Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is HabitFlow free?

Yes. You can use every feature for free and you do not need an account.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on your device. Clearing browser data deletes it, so use Export in Settings to keep a backup.

Will my data sync between phone and laptop?

Not yet. For now, export a backup on one device and import it on the other.

How do reminders work?

Turn on the daily reminder in Settings and allow notifications. Reminders appear while HabitFlow is open in your browser or installed on your phone.

How do I share my streak on Instagram?

Tap the share button on a habit and choose Instagram. HabitFlow creates a streak picture. On phones it opens the share menu, and otherwise it saves the image so you can post it as a Story.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. Open it in Chrome or Safari and choose Add to Home Screen (or Install).

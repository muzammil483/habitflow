Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

What we collect

HabitFlow does not have accounts and does not send your habits to any server. Your habits, check-ins, theme and reminder settings are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email app with a drafted message. Nothing is sent until you press send there, and then it goes to our email address.

Third parties

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • If you share your streak through WhatsApp or Instagram, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Your control

You can export, import or delete all your data from Settings at any time. Clearing your browser data also removes it.
